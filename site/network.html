Co-citation network

The 28 authors cited by the largest number of core papers. Two authors are tied when a core paper cites both; each author's three strongest ties are drawn, with width showing how distinctive the tie is. Click an author to see everyone they are cited alongside, and click a pair to see the papers that cite both.

Same construction as Figure 2.17 of the chapter: ties are normalised by the square root of the product of the two authors' citing-paper counts (Salton's cosine), node area is proportional to the number of citing core papers, and the three neighbourhoods and the positions are the chapter's reading of the map, set by hand, not the output of a clustering or layout algorithm. Positions carry no units.
